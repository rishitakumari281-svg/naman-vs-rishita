How to play

A / D: move (double-tap or Shift to run)   W: jump   S: crouch   E: dodge
Q: ultimate (Lv5 + full gold meter)
F: punch   G: kick   H: block   R: special
P or Esc: pause. Change keys in Settings, then Controls

On a phone use the on-screen buttons.
Win 2 rounds out of 3. Blocking cuts damage by 85%. Crouching dodges punches.
Your special recharges in about 7 seconds.
Chain hits for combos. Naman: run + punch = Blaze Rush. Rishita: block + punch = Petal Counter.
